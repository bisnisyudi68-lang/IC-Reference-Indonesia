🔋 iPhone No Power (X ke atas)

Step Awal Diagnosa

  1. Ukur VBAT di konektor baterai (normal 3.7–4.2V)
  2. Cek short ke ground (mode diode)
  3. Cek arus power supply
  4. Tekan power – lihat respon arus

Analisa Arus Power Supply

Arus Terbaca Kemungkinan Kerusakan
0.00A Jalur baterai putus / Tristar rusak
0.02A Stuck CPU tidak start / NAND issue
0.10A Drop PMIC rusak / Short ringan
0.30–0.50A Normal booting
1.00A langsung short Short jalur utama / kapasitor short

Mode Inject Short

  1. Lepas baterai
  2. Inject 4V – 2A limit
  3. Raba panas / pakai thermal
  4. Jika CPU panas → short internal
  5. Jika kapasitor panas → ganti kapasitor
